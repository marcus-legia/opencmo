Zernio is a unified posting API (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, Threads…), SDK @zernio/node. It fits the Post department's multi-platform scheduling. It must only post what the user approved, on accounts they connected themselves ("assist, never act").

Part 5

Skills to add from GitHub

Writer · No AI Slop

blader/humanizer

MIT · ~59.6k stars. Rewrites text to remove signs of AI writing, based on Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing". Can match a voice from a few paragraphs of the founder's own writing.

hardikpandya/stop-slop

MIT · ~18.3k stars. Banned phrases, banned sentence structures, and a five-dimension score (below 35/50, rewrite).

Video · ideas only

remotion-dev/skills

Remotion's own 12 skills: captions, render, markup, SaaS… The repo page states no licence, so read to learn (TikTok-style captions, per-word highlight); do not copy.

Already in the repo

apps/web/lib/cmo/skills: 7 in-house skills and 38 copied from marketingskills (MIT, Corey Haines), plus social-media-research-skills and claude-code-templates. Licences are in LICENSE.md.

Proposed wiring for No AI Slop
  1. Merge both into one file, apps/web/lib/cmo/skills/no-ai-slop.md, with the sources in frontmatter and the MIT licences in LICENSE.md.
  2. Load it into x_writer and planner when drafting (writing rules), and into checker when scoring (50-point rubric; below 35 goes back for a rewrite before Approval).
  3. Use the Brand Kit voice as the sample for humanizer's voice matching.
