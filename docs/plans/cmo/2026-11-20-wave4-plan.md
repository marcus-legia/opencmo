Launch is **30/11**. Wave 4 has two parts, and they never mix in one PR:

- **4a, before launch (lands by 28/11):** what a real founder and the Creem reviewer meet in the first week. Small,
  safe changes; nothing deleted.
- **4b, after launch (from 1/12):** delete the hidden TikTok / Instagram / slideshow / script code with its
  migrations, quote cards, and Gmail drafts if the owner says yes (below). Starts only after 4a is merged and the
  launch week is quiet: a deletion is not something to roll back during a launch.

## Owner decisions this plan needs (asked in the PR, default in brackets)

1. **Gmail drafts (4b).** `gmail.compose` is a restricted Google scope: an app that requests it from the public goes
   through Google's verification and a yearly third-party security assessment (CASA). [Default: no Gmail connect in
   wave 4; the Email tab keeps Copy and adds "Open in Gmail" (a `mailto:` / Gmail compose URL with the draft filled
   in), which needs no scope.] B5 / C6 build the default; the Gmail API path is written up only if the owner says yes.
2. **Self-serve export and delete (4a).** Still open since wave 3. [Default: a "Delete my account" button that asks
   for the email typed back and runs the existing purge; export stays by email to support.]
3. **Scan quality check on the real product (4a, ops).** Costs real reader and model calls. [Default: yes, on the
   owner's own Ferreto workspace and two test sites, about $5.]

## 4a. Before launch

1. **Phone layout for Sales and Feed** (canvas board "Sales on a phone"): one column; the thread list, then the open
   person full width; the reach tabs as a segmented control; sticky "Copy and open" at the bottom; the Context column
   folds into a sheet; Feed cards full width with their actions in one row. 390 and 360 px, no sideways scroll.
2. **States the review found still missing:** 100% of the plan used (what stops, what still works, when it resets,
   "See plans"); what a free account sees on Post and Feed after its free scan (one locked card each, the reason, the
   plan); the Feed's Sales card shows the reach level ("DM ready") like Sales does.
3. **Delete my account** (decision 2): Settings › Account, typed-email confirm, purge through the existing path,
   signed out to the landing with one line. Export: the support email, said on the same card.
4. **Scan quality on the real product** (decision 3): run the free scan and a paid scan on three sites (Ferreto and
   two test SaaS), score every card by hand (right person, real pain, fresh, reply worth posting), write the result
   in `docs/cmo/` with the numbers. A source under half "worth a reply" gets its query fixed before launch.
5. **Launch-week ops in code:** a `/api/health/cmo` line per source (reader up, last scan per platform, queue depth)
   that the owner's uptime check reads; failed-scan alerts to the owner's email (exists for jobs: confirm Sales).
6. **Copy pass:** every new wave 3 screen through `no-ai-slop` once more, as one read, with the canvas beside it.

## 4b. After launch

7. **Delete the hidden code:** TikTok, Instagram, slideshows, video scripts, Photos and Studio pages, their job kinds,
   routes, fixtures, e2e, CSS, contracts lines and loops; migrations that drop their tables and functions after
   moving any row a founder may still need (none expected: hidden since wave 2). The editor and the clip pipeline
   stay (Editor in the account menu). One commit per area so each can be reverted alone.
8. **Quote cards:** a post can carry an image made from a Library quote (the founder's brand look: colours, font,
   logo; the quote, its community, no name, no title, no link). Rendered with the existing card renderer, no AI image,
   no credit. Off by default per post; "Make a quote card" next to "Make an image".
9. **Email without a scope** (decision 1 default): the Email tab gains "Open in Gmail" and "Open in your mail app"
   with subject and body filled in, plus the opt-out line; "I sent it" as today. Gmail API drafts only if the owner
   says yes, as a separate plan.

**Out of wave 4:** the tracking script and Stripe connect, X and Bluesky as sources, Product Hunt work, the public repo
change, subdomains (editor., blog.).

## Global constraints

- Product rules (`CLAUDE.md`): assist never act; the founder sends every reply, DM and email; nothing unbuilt on
  screen; English UI; `no-ai-slop` on every word a reader sees; no fake numbers.
- Every write through an RPC with its pgTAP test; `scripts/dump-schema.sh` after a migration; founder-callable
  functions in `supabase/tests/182_authenticated_function_allowlist.test.sql`. Migrations from `20270202010000`,
  pgTAP from `347_`. Money only through the credit module.
- 4a migrations must run with the code already in production (add, never rename or drop), so the database can be
  pushed before the merge with no gap. 4b deletions may drop, and land in their own PR.
- Verify per task: typecheck, touched `.check.ts`, `npm run check:contracts`, `db:test` for SQL, a browser look at
  1440 and 390 (and 360 for task 1), the e2e specs you touch, `scripts/bundle-budget.mts` after a build when a client
  page changed.
