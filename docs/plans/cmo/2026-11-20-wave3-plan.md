## What wave 3 builds

1. **The Library is Sales' store.** Every scan saves, per conversation it keeps: a one-line summary of the post, the
   pain and evidence quotes, source and community, date, and later the outcome. Conversations are grouped into
   themes ("Chasing late invoices · 18 quotes"), with a weekly trend. Library page: themes with quotes, filter by source.
2. **Post writes only from the Library.** `plan_week` and `post_draft` pick the week's topics from the top themes and
   cite the source conversations; each draft stores and shows "Based on N conversations" with each one's summary (never
   its title). No Library yet: no automatic post; a brief the founder writes in the chat still works.
3. **The documents** (one page, Your business): Product · Strategy (ideal customer, pains, **where they ask**,
   positioning, **this week**) · Competitors · **Post plan** (topics from the Library, channels X/LinkedIn/Threads,
   rhythm; replaces Content Strategy) · **Brand** = Voice (pasted writing samples, how you say it is yours, words you
   never use; replies and posts read it) + Look (logo, two colours, one font from the site; screenshots; two image
   styles). Voice and words-to-avoid move out of Strategy.
4. **Sales scans on their own cadence** (Settings › Sales scans: Mon/Wed/Fri by default, weekdays, Mondays), not only
   when the week plan has a Sales item. **Today** puts people first: a `hot_lead` reason (Strong match or a thread
   under a day old) above posts, then follow-ups, then posts.
5. **Ready to reach out** (one check for every platform, from the post and its thread only, never a profile):
   buyer like the ICP · wants it now · warm (answered the founder, still active, or "DM me") · fresh (under 7 days) ·
   a way to reach them. Levels: Reply first (default) · DM ready · Email ready. **DM drafts** for DM-ready people.
   Email tab offers Copy only (Gmail drafts are wave 4).
6. **Following up**: "I replied" asks for the reply's link; each person has a status (replied, answered back,
   talking, signed up, customer, not interested); `reply-outcomes` re-reads Reddit and HN as today and adds Threads and
   Facebook groups where the reader allows (else the founder marks it); a nudge is drafted after 4 days with no answer.
7. **The funnel counts itself**: found → replied → answered back → signed up, per week and per source; shown in the
   left panel and Analytics. "Signed up" comes from the founder's mark and from UTM links (`?ref=ferreto&src=…`) when
   the founder's analytics reports them; no tracking script in wave 3.
8. **Research shows competitors** (logo from their site, price, what they sell, mentions from Sales scans). Add one by
   pasting a website, or from **Research's chat**: the founder asks ("find other tools people use to chase invoices"),
   the chat searches Reddit, HN and the web through the same Lurk API Sales uses, and returns names people mention with
   why, each with an Add button. No automatic competitor search (owner, 2026-11-18).
9. **Reaching out, the rest of the owner's rules**: DM tab "Copy and open" the platform's chat; "I sent it" moves the
   person to Following up; daily caps (at most 20 DMs drafted a day) keep it personal; no sequences.
10. **Emails of wave 3** (redesign plan, Emails): the **weekly report** on Mondays in place of that day's morning
   email (found, replied, answered back, signed up; what worked; what people said most; posts that went out), and the
   **strong-match push** right after a scan for a person who fits well in a thread under a day old.
11. **The screens the review found missing** (redesign plan, open items; canvas row "Gaps"): a paid account before its
   first scan (progress and "while you wait"), a scan that failed or a source that did not answer, Sales not set up,
   and **Settings › Tracking**: the founder's links with `?ref=ferreto&src=reddit|x|…` for profiles and posts, and the
   "Signed up" mark in Following up. Onboarding asks "who do you sell to?" while the site is read (the fit test).

**Out of wave 3:** the tracking script and Stripe connect (UTM links and the founder's mark count sign-ups), self-serve
export and delete (support email until then; owner has not chosen), Gmail email drafts, quote cards, the phone layout,
deleting the TikTok/Instagram/slideshow/script code (wave 4).

## Global constraints

- Product rules (`CLAUDE.md`): assist never act; replies, DMs and emails are always sent by the founder; nothing
  unbuilt on screen; English UI; `no-ai-slop` for every word a reader sees; no fake numbers.
- Privacy (PR #236 text): we keep a public post's link, community, date, a short summary and short quotes; no private
  messages; no profiles of authors; the author's business site only when the post links it.
- Every write through an RPC with its pgTAP test; `scripts/dump-schema.sh` after a migration. Migrations from
  `20270201010000`, pgTAP from `338_`. Money only through the credit module.
- Every prompt that writes for a reader embeds `NO_AI_SLOP`; new jobs get an eval case with the fake provider.
- Verify per task: typecheck, touched `.check.ts`, `npm run check:contracts`, `db:test` for SQL, build before the last
  push, a browser look at 1440 and 390 for UI, the e2e specs you touch.

## The shared contract (A1 pushes it first)

```ts
// apps/web/lib/cmo/library.ts (new)
export type LibraryEntry = {
  opportunityId: string; platform: SalesPlatform; community: string; postedAt: string | null;
  summary: string;              // one line, the post's point, not its title
  quotes: { kind: "pain" | "evidence"; text: string }[];
  themeId: string | null;
};
export type LibraryTheme = { id: string; name: string; count: number; thisWeek: number; usedInPosts: number };

// apps/web/lib/cmo/reach.ts (new)
export type ReachLevel = "reply_first" | "dm_ready" | "email_ready";
export type ReachCheck = { buyer: boolean; wantsNow: boolean; warm: boolean; fresh: boolean; dmAllowed: boolean;
  emailFound: string | null; reasons: string[] };   // one short line per check, for the screen
export type PersonStatus = "found" | "replied" | "answered_back" | "talking" | "signed_up" | "customer" | "not_interested";

// Post source, stored on content items written from the Library
export type PostSource = { opportunityId: string; summary: string; platform: SalesPlatform; community: string };
```
