Positioning

Founders need readers; creators have them. Cross Push wins on verified delivery (a live check of the X post, not a screenshot) and on taking no commission.

Who it is for

Solo founders launching a dev tool or SaaS who want 3 to 10 small X creators to post about it, and creators under 50k followers who sell sponsored posts.

Channels, in order

  1. X, where the product and its buyers already are.
  2. Reddit and Indie Hackers, where founders ask for distribution help.
  3. Product Hunt, once 30 creators are listed.

Weekly rhythm

Five X posts, one slideshow, two Reddit replies, and a video script when there is something to show.

Goal

20 creator sign-ups this week.
