Studio: the video department

Status: built (main at 4f70a59, 2026-11-08). Designed 2026-10-25, built in phases S0–S4 (P1–P6) and the CMO review (#169–#180). Markdown twin for agents: studio.md; the two say the same thing. Parent pages: ai-cmo.html, brain.html.

Studio is the editor plus the Video agent team. It makes every visual deliverable (shorts cut from a finished edit, slideshows, post images, and the AI long video) and hosts the founder's hands-on edits of their own video.

Where Studio sits now (owner, 2026-11-08). The AI CMO leads and the editor comes last: Studio builds from words the founder already approved (step 4 of the loop in ai-cmo.html §2), and the editor is where the founder fine-tunes by hand at the end. /video is to be hidden (owner decision): the full AI video (create_video) is built, and plan S2.4–S2.5 remove it from the web app, the landing page and the week plan; at 4f70a59 they still offer it. The code, the MCP tool and the evals stay. Every other slash command stays as it is.

§5

Design: one adapter, seven squads, shared tools

Scroll sideways to see the whole diagram.

Every squad has the long take's shape: LLM step → code reviewer (sends back once) → builder (ops) → renderer.

  • Callers: brain (work order + brief, through the order runner lib/cmo/jobs/studio-orders.ts), founder in the editor (chat, buttons, slash commands), MCP (founder's Claude with its own plans).
  • Studio adapter (lib/departments/studio/adapter.ts): plan → card → decide (brain cards go to the Approval inbox); every call under the founder's RLS session (a brain order mints a short-lived user JWT as MCP does); the order holds an allowance before its team calls a model (studio_order_hold); writes a deliverable with a status.
  • Squads: Long take, Intro, Explainer, B-roll + image, Shorts, Slideshow, Post image.
  • Shared: editor-core ops (only write path, Version history per write); generation (credit hold, spec hash, credit_orders for one-card jobs); moderation (Creem on the prompt before credits; Haiku vision + fal NSFW on the image or 3 frames after); render_document (one export per timeline); look check.
  • Inputs: marketing documents and Brand Kit; media library and uploads; transcripts (Scribe words, voice-over word timings); captured pages; style skills.
  • Outputs: deliverables kind = video | short | slideshow | image, cards in the Approval inbox, approved → Schedule. Editing the founder's own video produces a deliverable only when the founder marks the edit finished.

§5b

Charge before running in Studio

Owner rule (2026-11-05): a paid job shows one card with one price that covers everything, the founder confirms once, and the job runs to the end.

  • /video, one card (phase 4, c9a4f40). The storyboard card prices the whole video (keyframes, shots, voice-over, music). Approving opens one job (credit_order_open, kind = long_take); every piece starts at once and draws its price from the job (credit_order_draw); a shot names the keyframes it waits for with startAfter / endAfter (20270109030000_keyframe_chaining.sql), and a failed keyframe fails and refunds the shots that wait on it. The one-go cap (generation_active_limit, 20270109040000) guards load, not money. The job closes when the scene is placed, and the reaper closes one left open for a day.
  • Explainers: plan_explainer → preview_explainer → add_explainer (lib/agent/explainer-tool.ts). plan_explainer shows the storyboard (say, label, picture per beat), the direction and one price before any code is written: the render, plus writing and checking the animation, billed as used up to agent_3d_hold_credits(). Approving holds the render price in one job (kind = explainer, tied to its clip and turn by subject). preview_explainer renders stills of the scene code and a layout report, so the model looks at its own work and fixes the worst problem; it runs only after the plan was approved. add_explainer saves the previewed code, starts the render from the approved job (no second card) and places one video layer; the voice is made inside the render job. Since #180 the Assistant re-plans in every turn, fixes the preview's issues and ends with the voice.
  • Studio order hold (20270109050000_studio_order_hold.sql). A brain order holds studio_plan_hold() (5 credits) before the Director, the clip editor or the slides writer is called; usage is charged against it and the rest comes back when the order ends. Short of it the order fails as needs_credits and no model runs. The media of a brain video are held by their own job, confirmed on the storyboard card (kind = studio_video).
  • Review fixes (20270110010000_review_fixes.sql): create_generation takes the credit owner's lock before a shot's keyframe rows (a keyframe finishing while its shot was created could deadlock); an explainer job draws only for its own clip and turn; an explainer turn's tokens are capped at agent_3d_hold_credits().

Shorts squad

Scroll sideways to see the whole diagram.

Only from an edit the founder marked finished, in the founder's own project. No generation credits.

Chain: Segmenter (code) → Clip editor (LLM, agent shorts) → Clip reviewer (code) → Builder (make_short) → Renderer (renderShort) → look check. Built in lib/departments/studio/shorts/ (squad.ts, cut.ts).

StepWhat it does
Words with timesScribe words of the founder's video, or the voice-over word timings and beats of an AI video
Segmenter (code)sentence ends and pauses give the candidates; every range comes from here, never from the model
Clip editor (LLM)picks 3 (the default) of up to 8 candidates by id and writes hook, title and reason; one cheap call
Clip reviewer (code)starts and ends on a whole sentence (no name or number split); 15–60 s; the hook inside the first 3 s; no overlap; sends back once
Builder (make_short)copies the timeline, sets the workarea, frames 9:16 (face framing when the engine has it)

§7

Slideshow squad

A slideshow is a document of timed slides: same ops, renderer, export; no new runtime. Owner decision 2026-10-26: one editor core with a Slides view, not a second editor.

Chain: Slides writer (write_slides, agent slides_writer) → Builder (slide ops) → Renderer → look check.

  • Inputs: brief from the week plan, marketing documents, Brand Kit, captured pages of the founder's own site.
  • Writer: 5–10 slides; slide 1 is the hook; one idea per slide; the last slide is a call to action; no-ai-slop in the prompt.
  • Visuals: the founder's screenshots and captured pages first; an AI image only when needed, moderated, never with text inside the image. Text is always an editor layer.
  • Outputs from one document: MP4 (timed slides) through render_document and a PNG set (one still per slide) through render_stills; one slideshow card for both, with quick text edits per slide and "Open in Slides".
  • Editor: a project of kind slideshow; the Slides view (components/editor/slides) with the slide ops of editor-core/src/ops/slides.ts, so the Assistant and MCP get them too.

§8

Post image squad

For a post that needs a picture, a video cover or a YouTube thumbnail. A frame of the founder's video or a captured page with a title layer, Brand Kit fonts and colours, one still (build_post_image). It makes a one-slide edit in the job of the video it takes a frame from, and writes an image deliverable linked to its post (deliverables.post_id); it has no card of its own, the post's card shows the image. Built and tested in the acceptance runs, but nothing creates the order yet.
