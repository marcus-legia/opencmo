§5

Charge before running

Every paid job shows one card with one price that covers everything it spends, and runs to the end after one confirmation (owner, 2026-11-05).

  1. The card shows the plan and the estimate. Nothing paid has run yet.
  2. Approve → credit_order_open holds the whole estimate in one credit_orders row (kind long_take, explainer or studio_video).
  3. Each paid piece (a keyframe, a shot, the voice, the music, a render) moves its price from the job's hold to its own hold (credit_order_draw), so every existing settle and refund path works unchanged. A shot can wait on keyframes still being made (startAfter / endAfter, 20270109030000), so every piece starts at once.
  4. credit_order_close gives back what the job still holds. A job left open for a day (a tab closed, a crash) is closed by the reaper (credit_orders_reap, run from the sweep's turn reaper).
  5. A failed or blocked piece is refunded; credits come back only for what failed or was blocked.

Fixes from the review of this work are in 20270110010000_review_fixes.sql: one lock order for create_generation, an explainer job tied to its clip and turn (credit_orders.subject), and an explainer turn's tokens capped at agent_3d_hold_credits(). The same rule covers Studio orders (studio_order_hold, brain.md §9) and the Assistant's turns (brain.md §9b).

§6

Order of work

Ranked by the CMO loop (owner, 2026-11-08): does it make the loop work for a paying founder, end to end? Editor work comes after, unless the owner says otherwise. Almost every department phase is built; what is left is what stands between a paying founder and a working week.

  1. Business information: paid-first entry, setup cut to the website, the first week plan and drafts within minutes: done in S2.1–S2.2 (plan 2026-11-08); measured on a preview deployment by the owner.
  2. Writer: done: the week plan orders no AI video (S2.5); every post platform is built.
  3. Approval: no dead ends, one card per job (S2.6 partly done: Library, upload and transcript; the X card with no connected account waits on S3's SQL; S3.5–S3.6). Fewer cards a week gets its own plan.
  4. Video: slideshow, shorts from a finished edit and the Brand Kit are built; the post image squad has no caller yet (studio.md §2), and a finished long video gets no schedule slot. /video is hidden (S2.4).
  5. Post: Zernio live (owner: key, TikTok audit, price, terms); a provider that breaks shows on the Costs page (S3.1).
  6. Sales and Research: R0, the data terms, before new lead types.
  7. The editor, last: the long take's release, making /video cheaper and showing it again (its own plan).

History. Until 2026-11-08 this list was ranked by the editor strategy ("what lets us make and ship the next OpenCMO marketing video comes first"): Writer W0, Studio S0, Approval A0–A1, Brain B0–B1, Studio S1–S2, Schedule P0–P1 with Approval A2–A3, Brain B2–B3, Writer W1–W3 with Studio S3, Research R0–R2 with Sales R1, then the rest. All of those phases were built in P1–P6 and the CMO review (#169–#180) except Research R0.
