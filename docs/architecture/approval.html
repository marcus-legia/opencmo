Approval: the founder's department

Approval is where "assist, never act" is enforced. Every department raises a card; nothing goes out, and nothing paid runs, until the founder approves exactly what the card shows. This page says what exists, what others teach, and how one approval service serves the brain, the editor and MCP. Markdown twin for agents: approval.md. Parent pages: ai-cmo.html, brain.html, studio.html. Status: proposed.

Verdict

Good parts already built
  • Decisions run in owner-checked RPCs with locks (cmo_decide_post, cmo_decide_opportunity, cmo_decide_goal, cmo_mark_posted).
  • Outbound caps in SQL: 5 X approvals a day, logged in operation_log.
  • Every decision already teaches the brain: skip reasons become memories, edits are measured (cmo_draft_outcomes, similarity, edit modes).
  • Price before spending: the Assistant's priced cards and MCP's signed confirm codes.
What is missing
  • Three card systems that do not know each other: the CMO inbox (posts, Reddit, goals), the Assistant's chat cards, MCP's confirm codes.
  • No snapshot: nothing proves the approved thing is the thing that runs, and the time and the account are not part of any approval yet.
  • No card kinds for Studio (script, prices, finished video, shorts batch, slideshow) or Schedule.
  • No way to reach a founder who is not in the app: no digest, email or push.

Proposal: one approvals table and one approval_decide RPC under all three surfaces, a snapshot rule ("you approve exactly what you saw"), new card kinds, and reminders that open a card but never decide it.

Design: one table, one decide RPC, three surfaces

Cards keep appearing where the founder already is: the inbox for the brain's work, the chat for work the founder started in the editor, the confirm code for MCP. Underneath they are the same row and the same RPC, so every decision is counted, learned from and audited the same way.

Migration path
  • The existing RPCs stay; approval_decide calls them for posts, Reddit threads and goals, so today's behaviour does not change.
  • The Assistant's chat cards write an approvals row when raised and decided; the chat stays the surface.
  • MCP's confirm code becomes the row's id plus its signature, so a confirm can be found and audited.
  • pending.ts counts approvals rows instead of two tables.

6

Rules

1 · You approve exactly what you saw

The snapshot holds the content hash (JCS, so TypeScript and Python agree), the price, the time and the account. If any of them changes after the card was raised, the card is superseded and a new one is raised.

2 · Outbound and paid never auto-approve

Posts, replies, schedules and anything that spends credits always need a click. Only free, internal, reversible items may skip a card, and only if the founder turns that on.

3 · Few cards

Batch what belongs together (the shorts of one video, the slides of one slideshow, the posts of one day) and keep the daily count low; a founder who sees 40 cards a day stops reading them.

4 · Cards expire

A schedule card expires when its time passes; a price card when the price changes or after a set time (MCP uses one hour). Expired cards ask again; they never run late.

5 · Reminders never decide

An email or push opens the card. No approve button inside an email: mail scanners open links, so a link that approves would approve by itself.

6 · Every decision teaches

Approve-as-is, the edit and its mode, the send-back reason and the skip reason go to the brain's memory, as cmo_decide_post already does for X posts.

8

Learning from decisions

Approval is where the brain learns the founder's taste. Today X posts record the version chosen, the similarity of the approved text and the edit mode (cmo_draft_outcomes, classify-edits.ts), and skip reasons become memories. The same must hold for every card kind: a sent-back script, a skipped short, an edited slide. The Sunday summary turns repeated signals into a proposed correction, which comes back as a correction card (brain tier 0 memory).

Build phases

  1. A0 · One table, one RPC. approvals + approval_decide wrapping today's RPCs; posts, Reddit and goals move onto it with no visible change; pending.ts reads it. pgTAP for every transition.
  2. A1 · Snapshot + Studio cards. The snapshot rule; price, finished_video, shorts_batch, slideshow in the inbox, sharing the Assistant's card components. Needed by Studio S1–S3 and brain B3.
  3. A2 · Reaching the founder. Morning digest in the app, Resend email, web push, phone-first inbox.
  4. A3 · Schedule cards and opt-in autonomy. schedule cards with expiry; a setting for free internal items to skip cards. Outbound never.
