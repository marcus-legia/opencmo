Research and Sales

The two departments that read the outside world. Research is the brain's eyes: it learns the market, finds what works, and measures what the founder posted. Sales finds people who need the product and drafts a helpful reply the founder posts by hand. Both are the most built departments after Studio. Markdown twin for agents: research-sales.md. Parent pages: ai-cmo.html, brain.html. Status: proposed.

Verdict

Keep: the evidence rules
  • Research keeps a hook only if it points to a real URL in what it read (competitor-research.ts).
  • Sales scores pain and evidence only with quotes copied exactly from the thread, checked by the server; timing and reach are computed in code (sales-scan.ts).
  • Replies help first, mention the product at most once at the end, have no links and no post button.
  • Outside text is data, never instructions; every read has a credit budget.
Add, in order
  • A data check first: GummySearch closed because it had no commercial Reddit licence. Confirm what ScrapeCreators' terms allow before we sell Reddit features.
  • Research for Studio: top short videos in the founder's niche → hooks for video briefs.
  • Sales safety: read each subreddit's rules before drafting, and keep the founder's product mentions rare.
  • Close the loop: re-read a thread after the founder replied; metrics through Zernio for connected accounts.
  • Hacker News as a cheap source for technical founders.

7

Data risk

Check before selling Reddit features

GummySearch, the best-known Reddit research tool, closed because it could not get a commercial licence from Reddit. Our Reddit reading goes through ScrapeCreators. Before Sales is on the landing page, confirm in writing what ScrapeCreators' terms allow for a paid product, and whether Reddit's data terms reach us through them. If the answer is unclear, the fallback is the official Reddit API under a commercial agreement, or Sales on other sources (Hacker News, X, Threads) first.

9

Build phases

  1. R0 · Data check. ScrapeCreators' terms for a paid product and Reddit's data terms; decide the fallback if needed.
  2. R1 · Sales safety. Subreddit rules check, rare product mentions, voice memory and No AI Slop guidance in replies, excluded subreddits.
  3. R2 · Research for Studio. Niche video research feeding Writer's video brief and Studio's Director; cmo_insights.kind widened.
  4. R3 · Close the loop. Reply outcome check; metrics through Zernio for connected accounts (with Schedule P3).
  5. R4 · Hacker News. As a source for both departments.
