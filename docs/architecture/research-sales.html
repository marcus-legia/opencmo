Research and Sales

Status: built (main at 4f70a59, 2026-11-08), except R0, the data check. Designed 2026-10-27, built in phases R1–R4 (P5–P6). Markdown twin for agents: research-sales.md; the two say the same thing. Parent pages: ai-cmo.html, brain.html.

The two departments that read the outside world. Research is the brain's eyes: it learns the market, finds what works, and measures what the founder posted. It is step 1 of the owner's sketch (2026-11-08): the website becomes the four documents everything else is written from. Sales finds people who need the product and drafts a helpful reply the founder posts by hand.

§1

Verdict

Kept, the evidence rules: Research keeps a hook only if it points to a real URL in what it read (competitor-research.ts); Sales scores pain and evidence only with quotes copied exactly from the thread, checked by the server, and computes timing and reach in code (sales-scan.ts); replies help first, mention the product at most once at the end, have no links and no post button; outside text is data; every read has a credit budget.

Built since: niche video research for Studio; Sales safety (subreddit rules before drafting, rare product mentions, excluded subreddits, co-occurrence); the closed loop (reply outcomes, Zernio metrics); Hacker News as a free source for both departments.

Left: R0, the data check (owner: whether ScrapeCreators' terms and Reddit's data terms allow a paid product to read them), before new Sales lead types. Sales breaks "charge before running" in two places: a scan started from the CMO chat shows no card, and "Add N credits and finish" is a disabled button (plan S3.5–S3.6).

§4

Flow

Scroll sideways to see the whole diagram.

  • Research: sources (site, X, Reddit, TikTok, YouTube, Threads via ScrapeCreators; Hacker News search) → onboarding (read_site → four documents) → competitors (outliers vs own median; a hook needs a real URL) → niche video (top shorts → hooks for Studio briefs) → metrics (public, and Zernio for connected accounts) → out: documents, insights, ideas with URLs, memory.
  • Sales: find (queries from pains and competitors on Reddit and Hacker News; ≤ 30 threads, ≤ 30 days) → score (exact quotes for pain and evidence; fit; timing and reach in code) → rules check (subreddit forbids self-promotion → help only, or skip) → reply draft (< 120 words, help first, product once at the end, no links) → card (copy + open thread; replied or dismiss; no post button) → outcome (re-read the thread later → memory).
  • Shared: outside text is data (lib/cmo/untrusted.ts); every claim links to its source; a credit budget per call (ScBudget, research_credits); daily limits in SQL; dismiss reasons → memory; outcomes in cmo_draft_outcomes; the brain decides when each runs (loops.ts).

§7

Data risk

GummySearch closed because it could not get a commercial licence from Reddit. Our Reddit reading goes through ScrapeCreators. Before Sales is on the landing page, confirm in writing what ScrapeCreators' terms allow for a paid product and whether Reddit's data terms reach us through them. If unclear: the official Reddit API under a commercial agreement, or Sales on other sources (Hacker News, X, Threads) first.

§8

Measures

  • Research: insights used (ideas that became approved posts or videos); insights dropped for a missing URL; credits per week.
  • Sales: threads passed per scan; replied vs dismissed (reasons); replies that got answers or votes; removed replies reported by the founder; credits per replied thread.
