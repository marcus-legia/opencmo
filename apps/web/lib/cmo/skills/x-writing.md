# Writing posts on X

## The hook is the post
The first line decides whether anyone reads the rest. It states something specific:
- **Curiosity:** "I was wrong about [common belief]." / "We [did a specific thing] and [what happened]."
- **Story:** "Last week, [unexpected thing] happened." / "I almost [mistake]."
- **Value:** "How to [outcome] without [pain]:" / "Stop [common mistake]. Do this instead:"
- **Contrarian:** "[Common advice] did not work for us, because [the founder's reason]." / "I stopped [practice] and [result]."
Use the founder's real situation to fill the brackets. Never fill them with invented results.

## One idea, three formats
Pick the format that fits the idea, not the other way round:
- **Short (one post, under 280 characters):** one sharp observation or lesson. Shorter often wins; under 100 characters is fine.
- **Long single post:** a lesson with 3-5 short lines. Line breaks between thoughts.
- **Thread (5-8 posts):** only for a real step-by-step or story. Post 1 is the hook and the promise; one step per post; the last post ends on a takeaway or a next step, never a recap. Each post stands alone and stays under 280 characters including any numbering.

## Voice
- A smart friend who figured something out, not a brand. Write from what the founder actually did, saw or learned.
- Specific beats vague: a number from the documents, a named situation, a before/after. Avoid "streamline", "optimize", "innovative", "game-changer", "revolutionary".
- Short sentences, white space, no filler, no exclamation marks. The no-ai-slop guidance lists the stock patterns to leave out.
- Show, don't sell. Mention the product only when it is the natural answer to the post's problem. Roughly 5 in 100 posts are promotional.

## Rules on X
- No hashtags and no links in a post: our checks refuse both. No emoji walls. No "🧵 thread" unless it is a thread.
- End with something the reader can use or reply to — a question, a takeaway — never "check out our website".
- The first 30 minutes of replies matter: posts that invite a specific answer travel further.

## Before you hand it over
- Every number and claim appears in the documents or the research you were given.
- It sounds like the brand voice and avoids the words the strategy bans.
- It is not a near-copy of a recent post or of someone else's post: adapt the structure and the insight, never the words.
