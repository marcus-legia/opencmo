# Short video

## The idea
A good short video is one complete idea in 15-60 seconds. Pick: a strong opinion, a surprising number, a short story with a turn, a clear how-to step, or a contrarian take. Judge ideas on emotional pull, usefulness, story arc and contrarian angle; cut the warm-up talk in the edit.

## The first 3 seconds
Visual + spoken + on-screen text all hook at once. The first spoken line states the payoff or the tension. If the video starts slow, cut the start.

## Structures
- **Problem → solution:** name the pain, show the fix.
- **List:** "3 things…", one per beat.
- **Tutorial:** one step, shown.
- **Story:** setup, turn, lesson.

## On-screen captions
Burned-in captions, at most 2 lines on screen, 3-5 words per line, key words highlighted. Most people watch muted.

## Text per platform
- **TikTok caption (≤ 300):** one line that adds to the hook, a reason to watch to the end or save. 0-3 hashtags.
- **Reels caption (≤ 600):** first line is the hook in words; 1-3 short lines of value; 3-5 specific hashtags at the end.
- **Shorts title (≤ 100):** clear, searchable, what the viewer learns. No hashtags, no clickbait the video does not deliver.
- **Facebook (≤ 500):** plain and friendly, a question that invites a comment.
- **Threads (≤ 500):** the idea as a standalone thought, conversational, no hashtags.

## Rules
- Only the founder's own videos. The founder records and uploads them; agents never upload for them.
- Numbers in text must appear in the video or the documents.
