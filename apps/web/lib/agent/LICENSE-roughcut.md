# Rough-cut editing rules (adapted)

The `roughcut` guide in `guides.ts` and the retake finder in `retakes.ts` adapt editing rules from
the projects below. No text or code was copied; the rules were rewritten for OpenCMO's tools.

- [browser-use/video-use](https://github.com/browser-use/video-use): never cut inside a word, leave
  padding at cut edges, propose the plan and wait for a yes, check the cut edges before reporting.
- [Samin12/cut-video](https://github.com/Samin12/cut-video): how much pause to leave, fillers vs
  meaningful words, keep laughs and dramatic pauses, retakes by 4-gram similarity inside 60 s.
- [vincentventalon/claude-code-video-editing-skill](https://github.com/vincentventalon/claude-code-video-editing-skill):
  keep the last take of a repeated sentence; when in doubt, keep.
- [Abdalkaderdev/takecut](https://github.com/Abdalkaderdev/takecut): re-read the transcript after
  cutting to prove no sentence was lost.
- [palmier-io/palmier-skills](https://github.com/palmier-io/palmier-skills) (`ugc-editing`): the
  order of a talking-head rough cut (retakes, then fillers, then pauses, captions last).
